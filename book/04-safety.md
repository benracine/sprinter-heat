# Safety

Read this chapter before any procedure. Anyone helping you needs to read it too.

Jump: [System Design](#system-design) · [User’s guide](#users-guide) · [Builder’s guide](#builders-guide) · [Hazards](#hazard-register) · [The numbers](#the-numbers-that-matter)

::: {.danger}
**Carbon monoxide.** The exhaust must end in open air, away from the combustion-air intake and from cabin openings. Stop work if exhaust can reach breathing air. Never run the Hydronic D5S diesel heater in a closed garage. → [HAZ-002](#haz-002)
:::

::: {.danger}
**Fire and fuel.** Keep the fuel line off the exhaust. Keep the engine and heater off during fuel work. Inspect the fuel system before first fire. Do not light the heater to “test” a wet line. Use diesel EN 590; up to 20% FAME is acceptable; pure biodiesel is not ([SRC-009](#src-009)). → [HAZ-001](#haz-001) · [HAZ-008](#haz-008)
:::

::: {.danger}
**Scalding.** Tap water can track glycol temperatures of about 150–185°F. Use only the AM100-1LF thermostatic mixing valve on the Duda B3-12DW-20 plate heat exchanger, set near 120°F and labeled. Draw taps from the mixed outlet only ([DEC-008](#dec-008)). → [HAZ-005](#haz-005)
:::

::: {.danger}
**Shock and fire — Isotemp element.** The 750 W / 115 VAC element lands on Paneltronics **WATER HEATER** only—never on 12 V ([DEC-004](#dec-004) / [DEC-020](#dec-020)). Never energize an empty chamber. Confirm upstream GFCI/ELCI and cable route before first use ([HOLD 9](#hold-9)). → [HAZ-006](#haz-006) · [HAZ-009](#haz-009)
:::

::: {.caution}
**Hot glycol and steam.** Keep the loop full and bled. The heater limit is 2.5 bar; the WFT cap relieves near 1.2 bar. Never run the pump dry. Check the WFT only when cool. Treat running hoses as burn-hot. A lukewarm cabin heater while the burner runs usually means air or a kink (minimum flow about 250 L/h). → [HAZ-003](#haz-003)
:::

::: {.caution}
**Air lock, overheat, and lockout.** Use reinforced hose, avoid forced kinks, and put bleeders at high points. Do not thrash the EasyStart Timer off and on more than twice into a fault; clear locks with diagnostics ([SRC-009](#src-009)). After an overheat, wait until coolant is below about 70°C. Work on the exhaust only when the exhaust is cold. → [HAZ-010](#haz-010)
:::

::: {.must}
Keep glycol and drinking water apart; heat crosses only at the Duda double wall ([DEC-003](#dec-003)). Cap unused ports. Mix at most 50% propylene glycol, one brand only. Keep heater supply voltage about 10.5–16 V. Do not run overnight heat above about 5,000 ft without Altitude kit 22 1000 33 22 00 ([DEC-006](#dec-006)). → [HAZ-007](#haz-007) · [HAZ-011](#haz-011)
:::
