# Exhaust and Combustion Air

## Scope

Routing, diameters, lengths, and CO/fire constraints from Eberspächer hydronic manuals. Marine thru-hull/water-box practice in SRC-018 / SRC-019 does not apply to a van.

## Content

### Exhaust — Hydronic II kit (SRC-009 p. 24–25)

- Flexible exhaust inner Ø 24 mm, 900 mm, plus 300 mm tail with end sleeve, plus silencer.
- Whole exhaust run: **min ~200 mm, max ~2 m** (SRC-009 p. 25 figure).
- Silencer on L or Z bracket, always fixed to the vehicle (bracket torque 6+0.5 Nm; pipe clips 7+0.5 Nm).
- Tail should be much shorter than the heater-to-silencer run.
- Must end in open air; must not protrude beyond the vehicle side.
- Slope slightly down; ~Ø 5 mm drain at the lowest point for condensate.
- Mouth must not point into the airstream, clog with dirt/snow, or feed combustion-air intake.
- Clearance from fuel lines (plastic or metal), electrical cables, brake hoses, and other heat-sensitive parts.
- Recommended support spacing ~50 cm against vibration.
- Do not work on exhaust while the heater is running; wait until cool.

**Field note (SRC-005):** a stainless 90° exhaust elbow at the heater can reduce hanging height under a low Sprinter and give condensate a low drain point. Still obey slope, drain, clearance, and max-length rules in SRC-009. Confirm what is actually fitted on this van before redesigning.

### Combustion air — Hydronic II (SRC-009 p. 25)

- Draw from an area that stays ≤ 25 °C and is not spray/dust laden; not from the passenger compartment.
- Kit hose: double-wall sound-insulating, inner Ø 20 mm, **max 760 mm**, hose clip 3+0.5 Nm.
- May be shortened; cut clean so chips cannot jam the combustion fan.
- Opening always free; not into wind blast; not clogged; slope down with ~Ø 5 mm condensate drain if needed.
- Keep exhaust and intake from recirculating (silencer clearances min 150 mm / 200 mm — SRC-009 p. 25).

### CO / intrusion

HAZ-002: exhaust must not enter the interior through ventilation, warm-air intakes, or openings (SRC-009 danger callout).
