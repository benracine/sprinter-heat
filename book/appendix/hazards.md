# Hazard Register

Detailed hazard register for the practical guidance in System Design, User's Guide, and Builder's Guide. Manufacturer rules outrank blogs.

| ID | Hazard | Cause | Consequence | Prevention/mitigation | Verification | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <span id="haz-001">HAZ-001</span> | Fuel leak | Damaged/poorly routed fuel line or pump | Fire / injury | SRC-009 fuel rules: no exhaust contact, clamped lines (~50 cm), butt joints, continuous rise pump→heater | Leak inspection before first fire | OPEN — routing/clamps still need a look |
| <span id="haz-002">HAZ-002</span> | Exhaust-gas intrusion | Exhaust into interior, intake, or openings | CO exposure | Open-air termination; slope + drain; no recirculation (SRC-009 p. 24–25); never run in a closed garage | Routing check before first fire | OPEN — owner believes complete |
| <span id="haz-003">HAZ-003</span> | Hot-coolant release | Overpressure, failed hose, or air-locked overheat | Burn | ≤ 2.5 bar; WFT 5 L expansion/header tank cap ~1.2 bar; min flow 250 l/h; self-vent height; heater ~0.18 L — loop must stay full and bled (SRC-009) | Pressure/leak test; bleed high points | OPEN until commissioned |
| <span id="haz-004">HAZ-004</span> | Electrical overcurrent (12 V heater / fan) | Undersized conductor or fuse | Fire / damage | Fuse and wire from EasyStart Timer + heater diagrams (SRC-003 / SRC-009); fuse timer and fan separately after master switch (DEC-012) | Correct conductor and fuse selection | OPEN — wiring unfinished |
| <span id="haz-005">HAZ-005</span> | Scalding DHW | Unmixed Duda B3-12DW-20 plate heat exchanger outlet tracks glycol temp; mixer missing, bypassed, or set too high | Burn | AM100-1LF thermostatic mixing valve on the Duda B3-12DW-20 plate heat exchanger hot outlet (DEC-008 / SRC-024); range 70–145°F; set ~120°F; tap hoses from mixed outlet only | Land taps; label setpoint before showers | OPEN — taps not run; setpoint unset |
| <span id="haz-006">HAZ-006</span> | Dry-fire / shock on Isotemp Slim Square 4.2 gal heat battery element | 115 VAC on empty chamber; missing GFCI/ground; element in static glycol (DEC-007) | Fire, tank damage, shock; possible early wear | Fill chamber first; land on Paneltronics **WATER HEATER** (72313 / 9972313B — DEC-020); **upstream GFCI/ELCI** (not on panel); never a 12 V feed; isolate when servicing (SRC-022) | Fill-then-power; AC safety test (Q-009) | OPEN — GFCI/route pending (face known) |
| <span id="haz-007">HAZ-007</span> | Glycol / potable cross-contamination | Failed Duda B3-12DW-20 plate heat exchanger or wrong plumbing | Ingested coolant | Duda B3-12DW-20 plate heat exchanger double-wall is the only glycol↔fresh interface (DEC-003); Isotemp Slim Square 4.2 gal heat battery never holds potable (DEC-007) | Confirm fittings; never tee propylene glycol into water | OPEN — fittings still open on van |
| <span id="haz-008">HAZ-008</span> | Fuel takeoff quality | Pre-documented tap-in; owner once flagged concern | Fuel leak / fire | Factory **aux** connection used (lower risk than drilled tap). Still inspect routing/clamps vs SRC-009 before fire (Q-010) | Physical inspection with photos | OPEN — inspect before proceeding |
| <span id="haz-009">HAZ-009</span> | Wrong power for element | Treating 12 V panel slot as 750 W feed | Fire / damage | Element is 115 VAC / ~6.5 A on Paneltronics AC only (DEC-004 / DEC-020). 12 V is never the load | Verify AC branch before energizing (Q-016) | OPEN until HOLD 9 |
| <span id="haz-010">HAZ-010</span> | Control-box lockout / dry starts | Repeated off/on into faults; dry fuel line | Locked heater; failed trips | Prime fuel before first fire (SRC-005 tool; SRC-009 vent rule). ≤2 off/on cycles; EasyStart Timer diagnostics (SRC-009 p. 32) | First-fire checklist | OPEN until first fire |
| <span id="haz-011">HAZ-011</span> | High-altitude operation without kit | Sustained use above ~1500 m without air-pressure sensor | Poor combustion / faults | Kit **22 1000 33 22 00** wired per SRC-002; H-Kit on nameplate (DEC-006 / SRC-009 p. 32) | Install from garage before high camps (Q-015) | OPEN — kit in garage (identity confirmed) |
