## Implications for hydronic / furniture (2026)

1. **Heat retention:** Cabin already has a serious insulation + deadening envelope — diesel / Sure Marine loads less heat into bare metal than a raw cargo shell.  
2. **Access:** Book already requires **plywood access hatches** to metal for crash bolts ([HOLD S](#hold-s-sofabed-platform)). Insulation and panels are behind that story — hatches must reach metal without destroying the whole cavity pack.  
3. **L-track:** Side-wall tracks may help strap furniture / Isotemp cradle / cable chase — measure before ignoring.  
4. **Wheel wells:** Heavy Rattle Trap + insulation on rear wells — hose / cable penetrations need planned clear paths (freshwater tank already over **passenger** rear well).  
5. **Noise:** Rattle Trap helps with road noise and may slightly quiet cabin fan / pump vibration transmission — not a substitute for soft mounts.
