# How to Use This Book

Read in this order: [Safety](#safety) → [System Design](#system-design) → [The Numbers](#the-numbers-that-matter) → [User’s Guide](#users-guide) → [Current Status](#current-status-report) → [Builder’s Guide](#builders-guide).

| Stage | Chapter |
|---|---|
| Architecture | [System Design](#system-design) |
| Day-to-day (after Acceptance) | [User’s Guide](#users-guide) |
| Install | [Builder’s Guide](#builders-guide) |

Skim [The Numbers](#the-numbers-that-matter) as a short card, then keep going. While you build, return to Safety, [Controls](#controls), and [The Parts](#the-parts) whenever a HOLD points there. Do not light the heater from the User’s Guide until [Acceptance](#acceptance).

**Two finish lines.** Diesel cabin heat and diesel hot water can pass before [HOLD 9](#hold-9). The complete system, including Isotemp AC, needs HOLD 9. The install map lives at the top of the [Builder’s Guide](#builders-guide).

**Lookups.** For numbers and citations, use [The Numbers](#the-numbers-that-matter), the [Isotemp value study](#isotemp-value-study), [Sources](#source-register), [Decisions](#decision-log), [Questions for You](#questions-for-you-in-order), [Open Questions](#open-questions), [Hazards](#hazard-register), and the [Locked facts checklist](#locked-facts-checklist). Part names live in [The Parts](#the-parts). What is on the van versus still in the garage lives in [Current Status](#current-status-report). Part two is reference, not the work order. DANGER, CAUTION, and MUST boxes override surrounding prose.
